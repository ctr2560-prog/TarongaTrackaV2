Dear Student, Parent or Guardian,

As part of the upcoming twilight excursion to Taronga Zoo, students will take part in Evolve — a guided reflection on finishing school, delivered through Taronga Tracka, an educational app built for school visits to Taronga Zoo. It supports the mandatory Life Ready course.

About the program
What is Evolve?

Evolve is five animals told as five chapters of one story about leaving school. At each one, students watch the animal, write a short reflection, and film a short piece to camera. The clips are then joined into a single short film that the student keeps.

KangarooForward only
KoalaWhat I owe
GiraffeThe long view
LionWho I looked to
TigerThe territory ahead

There are no marks, no scores and no quizzes. Nothing a student writes or films is assessed or graded. The writing and the film are theirs to keep.

The experience
How it works
  • Students walk a route through the zoo, taking the five chapters in turn
  • At each animal they watch, write a short reflection, then film a short piece to camera on their excursion device
  • Clips are uploaded securely to Google Firebase — stored in Sydney, Australia
  • At the end, the app joins the clips into one short film, which the student can watch, download and keep
  • Students may also receive a Taronga Tag — a keepsake they tap with their phone to open their film and their writing again later
  • Schools sometimes print the koala chapter reflection as a certificate for a final assembly or reflection ceremony
Data & safety
Privacy
  • Evolve does not store student names. Students take part using a self-selected animal alias, such as Quoll or Bilby
  • Clips, writing and the finished film are accessible to the class teacher and authorised Taronga Zoo staff
  • The film is reached through a private link that includes a randomly generated code, so it cannot be found by guessing. It is not published anywhere and does not appear in search results
  • Footage and writing are retained so students can revisit their film. You may ask for a student's footage, writing and film to be deleted at any time by contacting the class teacher

Full details are available in our Privacy Policy: tarongatracka.com.au

One thing to be aware of
Writing that may be shared with younger students

In the giraffe chapter, students are asked what they wish they had known earlier in school. Taronga may share some of these reflections with younger students as advice from the year above.

  • Shared writing is attributed by year group only — for example "Class of 2026" — and never by name or alias
  • Everything is read and approved by Taronga staff before it is shown to anyone
  • If you would prefer this writing not to be used in that way, please let the class teacher know
Consent
No action needed

Students will take part in Evolve automatically as part of the excursion. If you would prefer otherwise, please complete and return the slip below before the date above.

Students who opt out of filming still take part in the full Evolve experience — walking the route, watching each animal, and writing their reflections. They simply will not film a piece to camera, and so will not receive a film. Please let the class teacher know in advance so this can be arranged on the day.

If you have any questions, please contact the class teacher.
