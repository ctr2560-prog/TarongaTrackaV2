Dear Parent or Guardian,

We are excited to let you know that as part of your child's upcoming excursion to Taronga Zoo, they will have the opportunity to participate in ZooSnooz — a wildlife documentary filmmaking experience powered by Taronga Tracka, an educational app designed specifically for school visits to Taronga Zoo.

What is ZooSnooz?

During the excursion, your child will film short clips of animals and enclosures at Taronga Zoo. At the end of the day, their footage is compiled into a personalised wildlife documentary — and delivered on a Taronga Tag, a keepsake they tap with their phone to watch their very own film. It is a unique, hands-on way for students to connect with wildlife and bring their conservation learning to life.

How it works
  • Your child films short wildlife clips using their excursion device throughout the zoo visit
  • Footage is uploaded securely to Google Firebase — stored in Sydney, Australia
  • The teacher reviews clips and the platform compiles them into a short documentary
  • Your child receives a Taronga Tag — an NFC keepsake they tap to watch their documentary anytime
Your child's privacy

We take student privacy seriously. Here is how footage is protected:

  • Raw footage is only accessible to your child's teacher and authorised Taronga Zoo staff
  • All raw video footage is permanently deleted within 48 hours of the documentary being compiled
  • The compiled documentary is delivered via a private, secure link on your child's Taronga Tag — not shared publicly
  • The documentary is hosted for up to 12 months, then deleted
  • Taronga Tracka does not store your child's name — they participate using a self-selected animal alias

Full details are available in our Privacy Policy: tarongatracka.com.au
