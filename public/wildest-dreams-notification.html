Dear Student, Parent or Guardian,

As part of the upcoming excursion to Taronga Zoo, students will take part in Wildest Dreams — a video-first experience delivered through Taronga Tracka, an educational app built by Taronga Zoo Sydney. It is designed for diverse learners, and it asks students to show what they notice rather than write about it.

About the program
What is Wildest Dreams?

Students visit up to eight animals. At each one they watch, choose what they want to show about it, and film a short piece to camera on their own or the school's device. The clips are then joined into one short documentary, My Wildest Dreams, which the student keeps.

There are no marks, no scores, no quizzes and no written work. Nothing a student films is assessed or graded. Students may speak on camera if they want to, and they do not have to: the app has picture buttons a student can tap instead, and a student can film an animal without appearing or speaking at all. There are no timers, every stop can be skipped, and animals can be visited in any order.

The experience
How it works
  • Students move through the zoo as a group, taking the animals in whatever order suits them
  • At each animal they watch, choose what they want to show, then film a short piece on the device
  • Clips are uploaded securely to Google Firebase — stored in Sydney, Australia
  • At the end, the app joins the clips into one short documentary the student can watch, download and keep
  • The film is also reachable later through a private link the teacher can share with the family
Data & safety
Privacy
  • Wildest Dreams does not store student names. Students take part using a self-selected animal alias, such as Quoll or Bilby, and the finished film is titled with that alias
  • Clips and the finished film are accessible to the class teacher and authorised Taronga staff
  • The film is reached through a private link containing a randomly generated code, so it cannot be found by guessing
  • The raw clips filmed at each animal are deleted after 12 months, once they have been joined into the finished film
  • The finished film is kept, so students can revisit it in years to come
  • You may ask for a student's footage and film to be deleted at any time by contacting the class teacher

Full details are available in our Privacy Policy: tarongatracka.com.au

One thing to be aware of
This program is built around filming

Unlike our other programs, Wildest Dreams is video from beginning to end — the film is the whole point of it, and there is no written alternative that produces the same thing. A student who is not filmed can still take part fully by operating the camera for a classmate and choosing what the group films, which many students prefer. Your child's teacher will arrange this on the day.

A student's face and voice will appear in their own film. The film is private to the student, their teacher and authorised Taronga staff unless your school chooses to share it with you.

Consent
No action needed

Students will take part in Wildest Dreams automatically as part of the excursion. If you would prefer your child not to be filmed, complete and return the slip below.

Students who opt out of filming still take part in the full Wildest Dreams experience — visiting each animal, watching, and choosing what the group shows — as the camera operator rather than on camera.

If you have any questions, please contact the class teacher.
